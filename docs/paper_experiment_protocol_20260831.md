# MLND numerical-experiment protocol (frozen 2026-08-31)

## 1. Research objective

The study identifies the largest geographically meaningful Nanshan crop for
which the mixed-delivery formulation (MX) can be solved to a relative MIP gap
of at most 1%, then uses that crop for formulation-matched network, cost,
service, and sensitivity comparisons.  The reported objective is modeled
platform fulfillment cost in CNY/hour; savings relative to the all-courier
counterfactual are neither revenue nor profit.

## 2. Frozen data and scale contract

- Source: the anonymized seven-day Nanshan order file already named in the
  canonical metadata.
- Candidate activity window: 08:00--24:00; demand window: 12:00--13:00.
- Demand is divided by seven one-hour exposure periods.  Every solve uses
  `demand_scale=1`.
- Scale changes only through concentric map crops.  An order is retained only
  when both endpoints are inside the crop; orders are never sampled,
  replicated, or multiplied.
- OD cells are 0.5 km and candidate-activity cells are 0.25 km.
- The six preregistered crops are H11, H24, H43, H68, H87, and H120, with the
  L/H/E targets and side lengths recorded in `scale_manifest.json`.
- Formal instances must be regenerated by the current weighted-K-means to
  nearest-active-grid implementation and stored under a new immutable result
  family.  Historical weighted-medoids metadata are not reused.

## 3. Frozen baseline model

- Formulation: `mlnd-unified-chi-epsilon-energy-v3`, result schema 5.
- Modes: home-delivery assistance (HD), mixed delivery (MX), and locker-only
  pickup (LO).
- Flight speed: 20 m/s; usable battery distance: 10 km per physical leg.
- All other baseline physical, capacity, cost, reliability, and customer-choice
  inputs are the defaults serialized in each `run_config.json`.
- `max_walk_km=1.5` is a paper-model coverage parameter, not computational
  screening.
- The large-instance preset retains eight backend origin lockers per displayed
  locker and ten general corridors per OD.  Such runs must state
  `screening_is_exact=false`; their gap certifies only that prespecified
  screened formulation.
- A complete-index check uses `display_origin_choices=all` and
  `general_corridor_choices=all` and must state `screening_is_exact=true`.

## 4. Solver and scale-selection rule

1. Fix data seed 42, solver seed 43, four threads, target gap 1%, barrier
   `Method=2`, and explicitly record `MIQCPMethod` separately.
2. Compare `MIQCPMethod=-1/0/1` under matched pilot budgets.  Select the method
   by the smallest final MX gap, breaking ties by runtime and then by the
   prespecified order `-1,0,1`; freeze it before managerial experiments.
3. Run the six screened crops for 300 seconds per mode in HD-to-MX order.
   A crop proceeds to long certification only if its 300-second MX gap is at
   most 5% and it has a nonempty feasible drone network.
4. Give eligible crops up to 3,600 seconds per mode; a 7,200-second extension
   is allowed only when the 3,600-second gap lies in (1%, 2%].
5. The main study crop is the largest crop reaching MX gap <=1%.  If no crop
   larger than H11 qualifies, H11 is reported only as an exact mathematical
   validation scale rather than being relabeled a large managerial case.
6. Independently run the complete-index H11 MX model to gap <=1% (maximum
   3,600 seconds) so that the full paper formulation has an exact optimization
   certificate even when the managerial crop uses screening.

All modes and scenarios report status, incumbent, best bound, relative gap,
runtime, work units, and solver-quality violations.  A time-limited incumbent
is never called optimal.

## 5. Core experiment matrix

### P0: required

- Six-crop computational scaling: HD and MX.
- Long-run certification of the selected crop.
- Same-crop HD/MX/LO comparison with identical data and baseline parameters.
- Complete-index versus screened H11 comparison.
- Rider tariff sensitivity: both rider tariff coefficients multiplied by
  0.8, 1.0, and 1.2.
- Drone-system cost sensitivity: facility, capacity, connection, route,
  flight, and fleet coefficients multiplied jointly by 0.75, 1.0, and 1.25.
- Customer-choice scenarios: locker discount 0, 1, and 2 CNY, accompanied by
  the analytical walking-distance-to-locker-share curve.  Discount affects
  utility but is not currently booked as a platform subsidy cost, so this
  experiment cannot support a net-profit claim.

### P1: robustness when the P0 budget permits

- H24 screening width 8/10 versus 12/16.
- Solver seeds 41, 42, and 43 for the frozen baseline algorithm.
- Battery-distance 8/10/12 km and swap-service time 2/3/5 minutes, each as a
  separately labelled operational sensitivity rather than a replacement for
  the confirmed 20 m/s and 10 km baseline.

Every sensitivity run must reach gap <=1% before point estimates are compared
as decision quantities.  Otherwise the report presents incumbent-bound
intervals and marks the comparison as unresolved.

## 6. Independent validation gates

For each formal result package:

- input, source, implementation, incumbent, log, CSV, and figure hashes match
  the sealed manifests;
- the reported gap equals `(incumbent - bound) / |incumbent|` for this
  minimization problem;
- objective components reconstruct the incumbent within 1e-5 CNY/hour;
- locker pickup + assisted home + direct courier fallback equals total demand
  within 1e-5 orders/hour;
- OD-to-corridor and corridor-to-physical-flow aggregation errors are at most
  1e-4 orders/hour;
- maximum energy violation is at most 1e-6 equivalent drones;
- every positive physical route uses open facilities and every battery leg is
  at most 10 km in the baseline;
- the output package declares whether screening is exact.

Failure of any gate excludes that run from formal tables until it is rerun or
the discrepancy is resolved.

## 7. Required paper outputs

- Scale table: crop area, demand, OD markets, H/L/E candidates, model size,
  incumbent, bound, gap, and runtime.
- Main three-mode table: cost components, service split, open/active
  facilities, connections, fleet, queue utilization, swap share, and chaining.
- Sensitivity and seed-stability tables with the same solution-quality fields.
- Nanshan basemap figures for the selected crop and the three service modes.
- Log-gap-versus-scale figure with the 1% line.
- Incumbent-and-bound cost comparison, service-composition chart, cost
  sensitivity charts, locker-choice distance curves, and locker-utilization
  figure.
- A technical report whose quantitative claims link to the saved ledger,
  summaries, and validation artifacts.
